    All Pokémon

    Chained PokeAPI endpoints

    1. GET /pokemon?limit=… — Pokémon list; each result includes the URL of the next endpoint in the chain
    2. GET {result url} (/pokemon/{name}) — confirms the Pokémon exists; types, measurements and stats
    3. GET {species.url} (/pokemon-species/{name}) — URL taken from step 2: genus, description and legendary status
